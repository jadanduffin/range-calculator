02 · How to use it

  1. 01

    Mark your position

    Pick Mortar or Artillery above. Open the map and zoom all the way in on where you are. Right-click your position and choose Mark Coordinates. The coordinates appear in the chat box. Copy them with Ctrl A then Ctrl C and paste into A.

  2. 02

    Mark the target

    Do the same on the target and paste into B. Zoom in fully each time so the coordinates are as exact as possible.

  3. 03

    Get the enemy range

    Press Enemy range. The page shows two numbers to set: RNG and Face. RNG is C, the straight line from your position to the target. The page treats the gap across the map and the gap up the map as the two short sides of a triangle, so A² + B² = C². One map unit is 100 meters, which turns C into meters.

  4. 04

    Dial the range

    Zoom into the sight. Move the barrel with W A S D for big moves and the mouse for small nudges until RNG in the sight matches RNG on this page. The range ticks are coarse, so take the nearest one.

  5. 05

    Face the target and fire

    Turn until the compass along the top of the sight reads the Face number shown above. Watch where the first round lands and walk the next one in with a click or two.

04 · Reach

Mortar L81
About 130 to 684 m.
Artillery SPH-2
About 745 to 2,660 m. High arc only under about 1,070 m.

These ranges come from player guides. The game publishes no official figures. Hills are not counted: a target higher or lower than you will land a little off, so be ready to adjust.

03 · Reading the sight

RNG Left side
Range: how far the shell will travel, in meters. Match it to RNG on this page.
MIL Right side
How steeply the barrel is tilted, in mils. A mil is a small slice of angle; 6400 of them make a full circle. More mils means a steeper barrel and a shorter shot. It moves with RNG on its own, so you never set it yourself.
Compass Top
The direction the barrel faces, in degrees: 0 is north, 90 east, 180 south, 270 west. It matches the compass on your normal screen. Match it to the Face number.

Mortar and artillery sights read the same way. Face assumes Y counts north on the map, which is what the community guides say, but it has not been tested in game yet. If a round lands on the wrong side of the target, aim by eye instead.

05 · Using the artillery (SPH-2)

  1. 01

    Bring a crew

    The SPH-2 has three seats: driver, gunner and a roof machine gunner. One person can run it alone, but it is slower.

  2. 02

    Park and buy shells

    Park on flat, stable ground. Press B and buy 155 mm high-explosive shells.

  3. 03

    Stabilize

    At your firing spot, scroll down to run the stabilization sequence. The status changes from Unstabilized to stabilized, and the crosshair stays put after each shot.

  4. 04

    Aim

    Set the weapon above to Artillery, then aim as with the mortar: match RNG and Face. Under about 1,070 m only the high arc (a steep lob) reaches. Further out, the flatter and faster low arc works too.

  5. 05

    Fire and reload

    Press R right after firing, then press the W A S D prompts in the order shown. Watch the impact and walk the next shell in.

The SPH-2 unlocks at career level 90. No guide explains how to switch between low and high arc.
